Document System: How Work Flows

Uploads, letters, e-signing and forms, start to finish. Green boxes are the finish. Red is a stop.

Shared by all four: the approval step. Each reviewer in turn can approve, ask for changes (returns to the author, not dead) or decline (written reason required; the author can rework and resubmit on a fresh list). Every decision is recorded permanently.
1. UploadsAny employee
  1. UploadPick file and document type
  2. Virus checkLarge files wait; infected ones blocked
  3. DraftAuthor can still edit
  4. SubmitReviewer list pre-filled by the type
  5. In reviewEach reviewer decides
  6. ApprovedAll steps passed
  7. Signing / auditOnly if the type requires
  8. ActiveThe official version
  9. ArchivedRetired, kept
  10. Disposal dueKeep-for period ended
  11. DestroyedAuthorised by an officer

Side paths: types marked "no approval needed" go straight from Draft to Active · deleted items wait 30 days in the Recycle Bin · replaced versions become Superseded · legal hold freezes a record and overrides any destruction · destroying outside the schedule needs a second person's approval.

2. LettersEmployee drafts, manager or officer finishes
  1. DraftWrite in system or scan in
  2. SubmitTo a checker
  3. CheckedMay be returned for edits
  4. Final approvalReference number, fingerprint and QR code created
  5. SigningIf the letter type needs it
  6. SentMethod and date recorded
  7. FiledPDF stored in the Archive

Incoming mail: a records officer registers it, gets a reference number straight away, routes it to a department and sets a reply due date. Then: received → responded → closed → archived. Overdue items are flagged. Anyone can scan the QR code, with no login, to check a letter is genuine.

3. E-signingSender sets up, signers sign
  1. New requestChoose a PDF
  2. Add signersColleague, a role, or an outsider by email
  3. Place spotsSignature, initials, date, text, seal
  4. SendOne by one or all at once
  5. SignSigner opens task or private link and signs
  6. Partly signedWaiting on the rest
  7. CompletedSigned copy saved as a new version

Side paths: a signer declines → document marked "signing declined"; sender cancels and resends · sender can cancel at any time · a request expires if nobody finishes in time.

4. FormsBuilder designs, anyone fills
  1. DesignFields, layout, letterhead
  2. Template approvedGoes through the approval step before going live
  3. ActiveAvailable to fill
  4. Fill inEmployee completes and submits
  5. ApprovalOnly if the template asks for it
  6. Reference numberCreated on final approval
  7. FiledStored in the Archive

Side paths: forms set to "no approval" are filed the moment they're submitted · a live template can be replaced by a new version or retired · employees track their forms under My Submissions.

No shortcutsNobody can jump an item to "approved" by hand.
Independent auditIf required, the final approver picks an auditor, who can't be the writer or approver.
Stuck approval?A records officer or administrator reassigns or cancels it.
All in one placeEvery finished item ends up in the Archive.
